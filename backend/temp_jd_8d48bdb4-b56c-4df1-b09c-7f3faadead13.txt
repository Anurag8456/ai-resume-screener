Career Progression
Starting as an SDE-1 provides a foundation for advancing to SDE-2, where engineers handle more complex systems, may lead smaller projects, and mentor juniors. With experience, one can progress to senior roles, including SDE-3 or Principal SDE, which involve strategic project management, system design, and leadership responsibilities
